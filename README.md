# HR Employee Attrition Dashboard (Tableau)
Built an interactive tableau dashboard analyzing employee attrition patterns across department, age, gender, education field, and job satisfaction to surface where and why attrition is concentrated.

## Overview
This dashboard analyzes HR data for 1,470 employees, with an overall attrition rate of 16.12% (237 employees). It breaks down attrition by department, age group, gender, education field, and job satisfaction to identify patterns worth flagging to HR/management.

## Key Metrics (KPIs)
- **Employee Count:** 1,470
- **Attrition Count:** 237
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
- **Active Employees:** 1,233
- **Average Age:** 37

## Dashboard Views
- **Department wise Attrition** — pie chart breakdown of attrition by department (Human Resources, Research & Development, Sales)
- **Number of Employees by Age Group** — distribution of workforce across age bins
- **Attrition by Gender** — gender split of attrition counts
- **Attrition Rate by Gender for Different Age Groups** — attrition rate trends across age and gender
- **Education Field wise Attrition** — attrition counts by education background
- **Job Satisfaction Rating** — job satisfaction scores cross-tabulated by job role

## Key Findings
- Research & Development accounts for the largest share of attrition (56.12%), followed by Sales (38.82%)
- Attrition rate is notably higher among male employees than female employees
- Life Sciences and Medical education fields show the highest attrition counts
- Job satisfaction scores vary meaningfully across job roles, with Healthcare Representative and Research Scientist roles showing distinct patterns
